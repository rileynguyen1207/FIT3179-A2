Chart 09 / Export concentration

Pistachio is concentrated in China; walnut and pecan are more spread

Five crops have numeric destination breakdowns suitable for area comparison. Each panel shows its own destination shares, regardless of its total export volume.

Small shipments reported separately

Hazelnut: China 71 t; New Zealand 3 t; Philippines, Papua New Guinea and Singapore each <1 t. Reported national total: 75 t.

Chestnut: a reported shipment below 1 t to Papua New Guinea.

Key takeaway

Concentration differs sharply between crops.

China takes about 92% of pistachio exports in 2025; pecan’s 2025 exports are split more evenly across its three largest destinations.

Reading the chart

Rectangle area shows a destination’s share within its crop. Equal-sized panels compare shares, not total tonnage. Almond and macadamia use 2024 data; other crops use 2025. Headers show the handbook’s reported totals and reference years. Areas use the sum of numeric destination rows; uncertain small shipments are listed separately.

Source: Hort Innovation, Australian Horticulture Statistics Handbook 2024–25. Source details

Data & methodology

Rectangle areas show each destination’s share of the sum of numerically reported destination rows within a crop. Almond and macadamia use 2024 destination data; walnut, pistachio and pecan use 2025. Equal panel areas do not imply equal tonnages.

The macadamia rows sum to 15,392 t compared with the reported total of 15,391 t; pecan rows sum to 159 t compared with the reported 160 t. Component values are retained, and panel shares use those component sums.

Hazelnut and chestnut are listed separately because quantities below 1 t cannot be assigned exact rectangle areas. No proxy tonnage is used. An absent destination is not assumed to have zero exports.
