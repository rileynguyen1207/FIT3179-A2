Chart 05 / Annual growth heatmap

The 2024 rebound was not shared by every crop

Annual change in production value, 2022–2025. Outlines mark the three crops that declined during the 2024 industry rebound.

Key takeaway

A stronger industry total can hide falling crops.

In 2024, almond, macadamia, walnut and pistachio recovered, while pecan, hazelnut and chestnut fell.

Reading the chart

Each cell compares a crop with its own previous year. Orange means decline; teal means growth. The colour scale is symmetric around zero and saturates at −100% and +100%. Labels retain exact changes, including pistachio’s +213.8%.

Source: Hort Innovation, Australian Horticulture Statistics Handbook 2024–25. Source details

Data & methodology

Annual change = 100 × (current production value / previous production value − 1). The 2021 figures provide the baseline for 2022. All 28 cells are computed from reported values; no missing value is filled with zero.

Percentages are calculated from the supplied rounded A$m figures, so they may differ slightly from separately published rounded growth percentages. Strong growth can follow a low previous-year base.
